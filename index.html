New — Begin With the End in Mind
Session 2 Framework
Sierra's Session 2 Framework
Decide your destination before you start driving.
When a question is asked, you have a breath — maybe two. Use that time to answer one question internally: Where do I want this answer to end? Once you know, speak toward it. You'll find your answers get shorter, cleaner, and more compelling — because you're not wandering toward a point, you're heading for one you've already chosen.
1
Take the breath
Don't speak yet. A pause reads as composure, not hesitation.
2
Set the destination
"My end is ___." Say it out loud in practice. Do it internally in competition.
3
Drive toward it
Answer, story, land at the destination. Stop when you arrive.

Performance Tips — Session 2 Emphasis
🎯
Lead With the Best Line
Session 2 feedback on two answers: you buried your most powerful line 30 seconds in. Before every practice answer, ask yourself: what's the most compelling thing I want to say? That's your first sentence.
😊
Smile on the Heavy Ones
Your warmth is real — but it needs to stay visible even on serious questions. Practice smiling at the beginning of every answer, not just the fun ones. It signals confidence, not flippancy.
🔢
Know Your Numbers Cold
Every 9 minutes · 3,000 goal · 100 registered in ~6 weeks · 60 Valentine's donors · $40K gala · 30 minutes to donate. Specifics are credibility. Run these numbers in your head daily until they're automatic.
⏸
The GPS Pause
The breath you take before answering is when you set your destination. Judges don't experience your pause the way you do. What feels like 3 awkward seconds registers to them as thoughtfulness.
🕐
Target: 11–12 Questions
Session 1: 9 questions. Session 2: 8 questions. More questions = more chances for judges to see you. Tighten answers by 10–15 seconds each and you'll get there. The "begin with the end" framework is your fastest path to this.
👗

Sierra has asked that you wear your interview outfit or at minimum your competition shoes for Session 3. Your body needs to know how to interview in those clothes. How you stand, how you sit, how you move — practice all of it.
